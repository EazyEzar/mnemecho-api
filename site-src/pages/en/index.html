Dutch pronunciation, scored sound by sound.

A Dutch teacher's ear for your app. Speech-to-text tells you the words. mnemEcho knows the sentence and tells you which sounds were right.

Send a Dutch sentence and a recording of someone saying it. Get back a score from 0 to 100, a verdict, and every word and every sound marked, with a short English tip on each sound to work on.

What you get back

A score and a verdict

One number, set against native Dutch speakers, and a verdict your code can act on: excellent, pass, marginal fail or hard fail.

Every sound

Each word, each expected sound in it, whether it was said right, what was heard instead, and a plain English tip, such as "the long ee in zee".

The wrong word caught

A learner who says the right sentence with one word swapped (maart for februari) is told which word, not given a pass.

Fair to every voice

A deep, high, male, female or accented voice is never marked down for how it sounds, only for which sounds it makes. We check this on every model change.

One call

curl -X POST {{api_base}}/score \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MNEMECHO_KEY" \
  -F "text=In februari is het Valentijnsdag." \
  -F "audio=@attempt.wav"
{
  "score": 91.6,
  "verdict": "pass",
  "worst_word": "februari",
  "words": [
    {"word": "februari", "ok": false, "wrong_word": false, "sounds": [
      {"target": "eː", "heard": "ɛ", "ok": false, "hint": "the long ee in zee"}
    ]}
  ]
}

Trimmed. The full response has every word and sound.

Private by default

Your audio and text are scored in memory and dropped. They are never stored and never used to train anything. We keep one usage line per request (time, status, audio length, score) to count your limits. Details in the privacy notice.

The learner app

mnemEcho began as an app that trains people to speak Dutch from memory: hear a native speaker, shadow them, then say the sentence from its English, with feedback on every sound. The API runs on the same scoring. The app is in private testing with a small group; write to {{contact}} to join.
