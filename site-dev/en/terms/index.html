API terms

Last updated 2026-11-09.

These terms add to the general terms (part A) at mnemecho.com/en/terms, for the mnemEcho Pronunciation API and its documentation ("the API"). By using a key you accept both. If a term here differs from part A, the term here applies to the API.

C1. Keys

Keep your API keys secret. You are responsible for all use made with your keys until you tell us a key has leaked; we then revoke it. Do not share a key outside your organisation.

C2. Fair use of the API

In addition to A4, you may not resell the API as a bare pronunciation feedback service without our written consent, and you may not use a result to make or support a decision about a person, such as on their education, health, employment, immigration status or legal rights. Results are practice feedback.

C3. API content

API audio and text are processed in memory and discarded after checking; see the privacy notice.

C4. Quotas, Pro and credits

  • When a limit or quota is reached, requests are refused until it resets or you add more.
  • Credit packs are valid for 12 months from purchase. Unused credits expire after that.
  • Refunds: ask within 14 days of a first Pro subscription or credit pack and you get your money back in full, as on the refunds page. After those 14 days, unused credits are not refunded.

C5. Versions

Within a major version (such as v1) we keep existing fields and endpoints working. A breaking change comes as a new version, and the old one keeps running for at least six months after notice. We may add fields and flags at any time.
