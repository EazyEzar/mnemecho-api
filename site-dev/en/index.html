Echo it until it's yours.

Dutch pronunciation feedback, sound by sound.

Pronunciation feedback for your app. Speech-to-text tells you the words. mnemEcho knows the sentence and tells you which sounds were right.

Send a Dutch sentence and a recording of someone saying it. Get back a match from 0 to 100 for that recording, a feedback band, and every word and every sound marked, with a short English tip on each sound to work on.

What you get back

A match and a feedback band

One number for the recording, set against native Dutch speakers saying the same sentence, and a band your app can use to pick its next step: move on, or try again.

Every sound

Each word, each expected sound in it, whether it was said right, what was heard instead, and a plain English tip, such as "the long ee in zee".

The wrong word caught

A learner who says the right sentence with one word swapped (maart for februari) is told which word, so the feedback is about that word.

Fair to every voice

A deep, high, male, female or accented voice is never marked down for how it sounds, only for which sounds it makes. We check this on every model change.

One call

curl -X POST https://api.mnemecho.com/api/v1/attempt/text \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MNEMECHO_KEY" \
  -F "text=In februari is het Valentijnsdag." \
  -F "audio=@attempt.wav"
{
  "match": 91.6,
  "band": "clear",
  "worst_word": "februari",
  "words": [
    {"word": "februari", "ok": false, "wrong_word": false, "sounds": [
      {"target": "eː", "heard": "ɛ", "ok": false, "hint": "the long ee in zee"}
    ]}
  ]
}

Trimmed. The full response has every word and sound.

Practice feedback, not a decision

A match describes one recording of one sentence: how close its sounds are to native speakers saying the same sentence. It is feedback for practice, in language learning apps, games and voice QA. It says nothing about a person's ability, and it is not an exam result. Our API terms forbid using it to make or support decisions about a person's education, work, immigration status, health or rights.

Private by default

Your audio and text are checked in memory and dropped. They are never stored and never used to train anything. We keep one usage line per request (time, status, audio length, match) to count your limits. Details in the privacy notice.

The learner app

mnemEcho began as an app that trains people to speak Dutch from memory: hear a native speaker, shadow them, then say the sentence from its English, with feedback on every sound. The API runs on the same feedback engine. The app has its own page.
